## The pitch

> PaintWatch is a premium app that hires a certified professional to watch your paint dry and emails you a daily progress report with a dryness percentage and a photo of the unchanged wall, for just $9.99 a month.

211 characters. One sentence. A product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a function nobody on earth needs: a certified professional, paid to watch paint dry, reporting daily on a wall that has not changed.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the pitch above to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 211 bytes, UTF-8, no trailing newline. Run it with Node 22.18 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```
